# 🎵 Cave of Songs

**Cave of Songs** is a rhythm exploration game made in **Unity**.  
You’re lost in a mysterious cave filled with songs and rhythms — interact with strange objects, uncover secrets, and unlock music as you explore.

## 🕹️ Game Overview
- A **music-driven experience** where rhythm and exploration blend
- Interact with mysterious objects tied to sound
- Discover and unlock musical elements throughout the cave
- Atmospheric game world that responds to your exploration

## 🚀 How to Play
Download or play in the brower from itch.io:  
  [Windows](https://muuum.itch.io/cave-of-songs) 
  [Browser](https://muuum.itch.io/cave-of-songs-web)

*(Instructions and controls are provided in-game.)*
